Master's in Disability Studies

A partnership of Federal University of Lafia and Deaf-in-Tech

Federal University of Lafia, through its Center for Disability Studies, runs the Master's programme in Disability Studies in partnership with Deaf-in-Tech, the inclusion programme of Data-Lead Africa. Students register on the student portal, sit their examinations in the browser, and results reach the university the moment a paper is submitted.

What the partnership does

Teaching is delivered by Deaf-in-Tech and assessment results are reported back to the university. Sessions are delivered in sign language and every assessment is written, so nothing depends on hearing.

Practical training

Built around the tools students will use in real analysis work, not around a textbook.

Credible assessment

Every student sits the same paper on the same clock, marked on the server, not by hand.

Straight to the university

Each result carries the student's name, matriculation number and paper.

Works on any device

An ordinary browser on a phone, tablet or laptop. Nothing to install, and no audio.

Questions students ask

Who is this examination for?

Students taking the Deaf-in-Tech component of the Master's programme in Disability Studies at the Federal University of Lafia.

What do I need in order to sit it?

A device with a web browser, a stable internet connection and your matriculation number. There is nothing to install, and the whole assessment is written, so there is no audio at any point.

Can I change an answer after choosing it?

Yes, until you submit. Use the numbered question grid to return to any question. Once you submit you cannot return to the paper.

What happens if the timer runs out?

Your paper is submitted automatically with the answers recorded at that moment. Answers are saved as you go, so nothing is lost if your connection drops.
