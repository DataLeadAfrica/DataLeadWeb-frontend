Before you register

Do I need coding or data experience?

No. The programme starts from the basics and builds up to job-ready skills. Beginners are welcome.

Is it online or onsite?

Both. Join live online from anywhere, or in person at our Abuja hub.

What laptop do I need?

Any laptop running Windows, macOS or Linux with at least 4GB RAM and about 250GB storage, plus a stable internet connection.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes, a Data-Lead Africa certificate, plus a portfolio-ready capstone project you can show employers.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. You can spread payment as three payments of ₦100,000 over the term (₦300,000 total), or pay ₦250,000 once.

Is there an NYSC discount?

Yes. Serving corps members in Nigeria pay ₦150,000. You will show proof of NYSC at onboarding.
