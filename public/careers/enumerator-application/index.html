Field Enumerator Application

Data-Lead Africa is a research consulting firm with over a decade of experience in data collection, research, monitoring and evaluation, and data analytics. Our expertise spans Conflict and Peacebuilding, WASH, FSL, Education, Child Protection, Agriculture, Health, Economics, and Nutrition.

We are seeking experienced Field Enumerators for an endline evaluation in Lagos and Ekiti States in August 2026. The study assesses the impact of a donor-led intervention in climate-smart agricultural practices.

Field Enumerators must be resident in the state where data collection takes place and have a working knowledge of its cultural context.
