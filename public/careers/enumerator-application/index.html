Field Enumerator, Lagos and Ekiti

Where Lagos and Ekiti States When August 2026 Takes About 5 minutes

Data-Lead Africa is recruiting experienced field enumerators for an endline evaluation of a climate-smart agriculture programme in Lagos and Ekiti.

This is grassroots work. You will spend your days in rural communities, sitting with the farmers, traders and households who keep the food chain moving, earning their trust and getting their answers down faithfully.

You must live in the state of data collection. The ability to read and write in Yoruba is required, and knowledge of the Ekiti dialect is an added advantage. Have your CV ready before you start.
