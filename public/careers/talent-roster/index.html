Join our network of research and development professionals

Data-Lead Africa works with researchers, evaluators, data professionals, sector specialists, field teams, trainers and consultants to deliver assignments across Africa, from country-level work to multi-country and regional engagements.

Opportunities include
Research and Evaluation Data Analytics and Statistics Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning Field Research Technical and Sector Consultancy Data Visualisation and Digital Solutions Training and Facilitation Programme and Project Support

Tell us about your expertise, experience, location and availability. When an opportunity matches your profile, our team may contact you to discuss the assignment. It takes about 5 minutes; please have your CV ready as a PDF or Word file.

Joining the talent roster is not an application for a specific vacancy and does not guarantee an engagement. If you are interested in an advertised position, please apply directly through the relevant vacancy.
