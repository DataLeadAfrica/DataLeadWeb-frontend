Questions applicants ask

Do I need any design experience to join?

No. The bootcamp starts with the fundamentals, so complete beginners can follow it. People who already design are welcome too, and the class covers professional software rather than beginner tools only.

What software will I learn?

Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator and CorelDRAW. These are the tools used in print shops, agencies and in-house design teams, so what you learn transfers directly to paid work.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. You receive a certificate once you complete the course. Attendance matters, because the certificate reflects work you actually did in class.

How long is the training and where does it hold?

It runs for one month, four weeks in total, and it is fully on-site rather than online. Training begins on Monday 5 October 2026.

Does registering confirm my place?

Not on its own. Places are limited, so every registration is reviewed and we contact applicants about the next step.
